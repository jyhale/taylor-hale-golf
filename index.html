The Story So Far

Two events into her freshman campaign, Taylor is trending the right way. At Quail Valley she carded an even-par second round — the low round of anyone on the St. John's roster that day — and finished T22. At Princeton she cut her opening round nearly in half by day two (+9 → +4) before the final round was washed out by a Nor'easter. Her scoring average sits at 77.20 (about +5.6 per round, across par-72 Quail Valley and par-71 Springdale), with the arrow pointing down.

Taylor joins a program on a clear upswing. After finishing 6th at the 2025 BIG EAST Championship, the Red Storm broke through last spring with a runner-up finish at the 2026 BIG EAST Championship — their best since the 2005 title and the first-ever runner-up in the event's history (since 2003). They set a new program single-round scoring record (1-under 287) on the final day, falling only to Xavier, winners of seven straight conference titles. The 2025–26 team also won the St. John's Invitational and placed runner-up in three events.
Returning core now on Taylor's team: Lily Huynh (T7 at the 2026 BIG EAST), Pihla Pennanen (T7), and Kirsten Lee (T15 as a freshman). Next real measuring stick: the BIG EAST Championship, Apr 24–26, 2027 — Riverton Pointe, SC.

Better finishes — but softer fields. The 4th-vs-13th opener gap is mostly the schedule: this year's Quail Valley was a 9-team regional field, while last year's Nittany Lion was a Penn State–hosted, Big Ten–caliber field on a much harder course. The clean like-for-like is Princeton — same course, same Ivy+ field — where they went 7th both years. So against equal competition the team is holding serve, not surging — still a quiet win given they replaced graduated leaders Kelly Wu (All-BIG EAST) and Cathilyn Lee, with sophomore Laura Mizlerova jumping T17 → T10 at Princeton.
The real test is ahead. Last season was a slow fall that peaked into a historic spring (BIG EAST runner-up). With this fall's early fields on the softer side, the honest verdict waits for the tougher spring slate — above all the BIG EAST Championship, where the field is fixed and Xavier awaits.
